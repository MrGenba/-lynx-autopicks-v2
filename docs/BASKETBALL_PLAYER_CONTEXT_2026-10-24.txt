NBA/WNBA: opponent adjustments and workload context
===================================================

Active generation: bk-player-context-2. This is a user-requested functional
change for future projections, not a statistically established improvement.
Old forecasts, decisions, prices and balances remain immutable. Old context
generations stay available for both settlement and prospective comparison.

Individual forecasts apply opponent two/three-point conversion allowed,
assists per possession and offensive/defensive rebound opportunities inferred
from missed field goals. The player's observed shot mix and ORB/DRB shares
determine the adjustment. Opponent samples: last 20 verified games, minimum 8;
league prior: minimum 100 team records; factors bounded to 0.92--1.08 and
shrunk with 400/300 attempts or 1000 possessions. Current season is preferred;
previous season and regular-season/playoff pooling are explicitly labelled.
No optical WNBA tracking, individual defensive assignments or measured xFG
are invented. Expected makes are historical rates adjusted for exposure.

Minutes use recent same-team games, confirmed role when known, overtime
normalization and comparable rotations. Two missed team games flag a possible
return (not an injury diagnosis); the first three return games use observed
post-return minutes and retain an uncertainty block. Staff-reported returns
also increase uncertainty. Relevant teammate absences/returns require at least
five comparable rotation observations; otherwise value remains blocked.
Team minutes are capped at 240 NBA / 200 WNBA without inventing missing players.
Fresh rosters and recent team appearances can add players without old forecasts.

Sources: official injury reports, confirmed/expected RotoWire lineups, and the
public RotoWire news lead (subscription analysis is excluded). The last two
calendar days of captured news are retained on the VPS so a restriction does
not disappear when a story leaves the main feed. This is coverage of captured
public news, not every staff interview. A numeric cap requires an explicit
named-player statement, staff attribution and a matching game date. Conflicting
caps use the smaller number and block value. Date-only news never establishes
medical clearance. Qualitative restrictions without a cap also block value.
Published minute ranges are indicative, not calibrated confidence intervals.

Runtime: worker and n8n on the VPS; dedicated basketball PostgreSQL/PostgREST.
No local PC runtime or baseball Supabase storage required. Context and analysis
refresh every five minutes. Existing EV >= 10%, quote freshness, paired market,
confirmed lineup, uncertainty and injury gates remain. Confirmed lineups or a
changed availability/news revision trigger a separate reanalysis. Telegram
previews display minute ranges, cap source/date and opponent adjustment status.
