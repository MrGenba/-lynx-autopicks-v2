Player context version 4
========================

This change reconstructs the lost development work from the verified live
version 3 bundles, rather than treating the older repository as production.
No increased accuracy or profitability is established by this deployment.

The projected starting/bench role is resolved from the same current lineup
and public named-player news for both minutes estimation and pick display.
Unknown roles, conflicting news, insufficient role/rotation samples, uncertain
teammates and returns with uncertain workloads hold publication.

Comparable rotations require five observations in the current availability
pattern and five contrasting observations with the same role. A full boxscore
alone cannot classify a player outside a verified same-team tenure as absent.
Recipients can gain, lose or retain minutes. Missing teammate minutes are not
assigned automatically to a replacement. Effects are bounded and shrunk.

When sufficient data exist, shot attempts and rebound/assist production rates
are compared per minute and normalized for historical team pace. Points use
pooled shot conversion and changes in attempts; hot shooting is not treated as
new opportunity. WNBA boxscore proxies are never labelled tracking data.
The advanced input's attack/pace effects, opponent defense and overtime mixture
are retained. The new minute/production adjustment occurs before defense and
the overtime mixture, which are each applied once. Explicit caps survive OT.

The injury-exit evidence table is separate from official boxscores. Only an
explicit named, dated injury exit with no return, linked to a unique completed
game and observed before the forecast, may exclude that exposure from new-model
training. Low minutes alone are not evidence. Actual stats and outcomes remain
unchanged and still count in evaluation. No historical forecast is rewritten.

Protocol: bk-player-context-prospective-4. Baseline: bk-player-context-3.
First 200 eligible future paired games per league after registration, including
discarded candidates. Half-point O/U, maximum 12% overround, game-level Brier
differences and fixed 2000-resample bootstrap. Both 95% upper bounds must be
negative against the previous sealed model and no-vig market. Prior protocols
continue separately. No automatic model promotion or reuse of inspected tests.

Upgrade entry point: basketball/upgrade_workflows.js takes a fresh live export
and changes only the relevant modules. Preserve the live runtime suffix and
all nodes, connections, credentials, schedules, notification destinations and
settings. Do not rebuild existing workflows with old deployment config files.
The strict filtered-channel gates remain probability > 0.60 and edge over
1/odds > 0.20. Confirmed-lineup reanalysis remains disabled. Deduplication and
compact pick formatting are retained for both NBA and WNBA.

Worker deployment uses the existing persistent basketball-data volume and a
last startup layer in player-context-4. It updates context_capture.py, injury_exit.py and
context_sources.py, preserving Tor, Winamax's original connection and Proton
isolation. The official injury PDF reader also preserves an explicit reason
continued at the start of the next page, instead of invalidating the whole report.
No shared baseball service rebuild/restart is necessary. Before a
future base-image rebuild, reconcile existing persistent source patch layers
with the chosen image; the repository had been behind those live patches.

Recovery assets (private, never version): work/recovery in the maintenance
chat, plus /app/basketball-data/player-context-4/recovery on the VPS. A full
database dump, original workflow exports, source snapshots and startup backup
precede the update. Restore workflow exports to roll back executable logic;
restore the worker/startup files for capture rollback. Additive evidence schema
and historical forecasts can remain; do not erase observations to roll back.
